| F1 | **OSM tiles and offline use.** The OSM policy says "Offline use is not permitted on tile.openstreetmap.org" and asks caches to honour `Cache-Control`, `Expires` and `ETag`. Option B: keep a worker cache but with `maxAgeSeconds` ≤ the server's max-age and no offline promise. Option C: a tile provider that allows offline use, or self-hosted tiles (T1 fallback) | TRD §13.3, §15 (CSP), T1 | **Built 2026-10-20 (W7-6) as TRD §13.3 says**: cache first, 2,000 entries, 30 days, 200 answers only, `crossOrigin` tiles, viewed tiles only (no prefetch), emptied at logout. **Still open on the policy letter** (offline use is not permitted). **Decided 2026-10-20 (W7-9): self-host a Protomaps extract** (option C, self-hosted) instead of a paid provider (Stadia allows limited caching only for mobile apps, 100 MB a device, and MapTiler's Cloud terms say nothing). Plan: `pmtiles extract` of the Protomaps daily build for the countries in scope (`--region`, `--maxzoom` 14 or 15), checked with b3sum and kept on our own host (Protomaps asks not to hotlink; ODbL attribution stays); `protomaps-leaflet` 5.1.0 in `MapView.vue` (maintenance mode, but it has no worker and no `blob:`, so the CSP holds; MapLibre GL would need its CSP worker build and a rewrite of the clustering); the service worker's OSM route becomes a byte-range cache for the one file (a PMTiles `Source` that stores each range in `mms-tiles`, emptied at logout).
