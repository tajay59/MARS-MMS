# MARS-MMS for technicians: quick reference

One page, for the training session (W23-5). Button names are the app's own, in **bold**.

## Before the first visit

1. **Install the app.** iPhone: open the site in **Safari**, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**, and open it from the home screen from then on (a Safari tab and the installed app keep separate data). Android: open it in **Chrome** and tap **Install** (or the menu, then **Install app**).
2. **Your phone needs a passcode.** The app stores your visits on the phone, and the passcode protects them.
3. **Set an offline PIN.** **My profile**, then **Offline PIN**, then **Set a PIN**. With no signal, the app opens with this PIN instead of your password. Five wrong PINs and it asks you to sign in with a connection; your work stays on the phone.
4. **Used the staging app before?** Open it, let it **Sync now** until nothing is waiting, then install the production app. Nothing moves from one to the other.

## Before you leave signal

- Open the visit and tap **Prepare for field**. Wait for **Ready offline**: the station, its documents and its configuration files are now on the phone.
- A visit you start without preparing asks first: **Prepare now** or **Start anyway**.
- Each station on a visit has **one owner**. Only the owner changes that station's board. If you need to work on someone else's station, ask an admin to change the owner before you go.

## At the station (no signal is fine)

- **Start visit**, then **Arrive** at each station and **Depart** when you leave it.
- Scan the station's label with the app's **Scan** tab to open it.
- The board holds the work: **Note**, **Task** (with Before and After photos), **Recommendation**, **Config** (Before and After files). Everything is saved on the phone at once. A photo shows **On phone** until it has gone up.
- **End visit** when the work at every station is done. The app goes to **Sync**.

## Getting it to the server

- Sync runs by itself when you have signal, when you open the app, and every 5 minutes. **Sync now** starts it at once.
- **Keep the app open and the screen on while it syncs**, above all on an iPhone: it cannot sync in the background. Large videos take a while on a weak signal.
- The phone deletes a visit only after the server has confirmed every change and file.

## When Sync says "Needs attention"

Nothing is lost: your copy stays on the phone. The common reasons:

| You see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner changed | Someone else owns the station now. Tap **Export your work** and send the file to them or an admin |
| Changed on another device | The server kept the other version. Export yours if it matters |
| Report is final | Ask an admin to reopen the report, then sync again |
| Visit cancelled | An admin has been told. Export your work |
| Could not be saved | Sync again later. If it stays, export and tell an admin |

When you have exported it, **Done with this** removes it from the phone.

## After the visit

- The visit report starts as a draft. Edit it and sign it; the lead then taps **Finalize**. Reminders come after 2 and 5 working days if it is still not final.
- Only the shared items on the board print in the report. The client sees the report only once it is final.

Problems with the app: tell the admin running the pilot, with the time, your phone and the visit's reference.
