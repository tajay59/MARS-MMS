## 3. Decisions (proposed W17-1 to W17-14)

1. **Only shared elements print.** A final report is one PDF for everyone, and a client may download it (rule 6). So the narrative prefill and the PDF use the elements with `shared = true`, and the follow-up notes (clients already see them, W10). The editor shows staff every element, with a "Staff only, not in the report" mark on the others, and "Fix on the board" beside each fact. This also corrects the W16 draft, which prints everything. A draft and its final therefore look the same.
2. **Editing is by field, with a version.** `PATCH /api/v1/reports/{id}/content` takes `{content_version, narrative?: {summary?, recommendations?, follow_up?, stations?: [{station_id, work_performed}]}, media_selection?}` and answers the new record. A stale `content_version` is 409 `content_changed` with the current record, so two editors never overwrite each other silently; an identical save changes nothing and does not move the version (idempotent by content). Draft only; attending staff and admins (viewers read). Text is plain: stored as typed, printed escaped.
3. **A signature is bound to a hash.** `content_hash` = SHA-256 of the canonical JSON (sorted keys, UTF-8) of `content` (facts, narrative, selection) at that moment. A signature stores the hash it was given against. **Any change that alters the hash clears the valid signatures** (narrative edit, photo choice, or a facts refresh that really changed a fact). A facts refresh that reads back identical facts changes nothing and keeps the signatures.
4. **Cleared signatures are kept, flagged.** `signatures` rows are never deleted: a cleared one gets `cleared_at` and `cleared_by` (`edit`, `facts`, `reopen`). Only valid ones count. The audit trail (rule 10) has `report.signed` and `report.signatures_cleared` (ids and counts, never the image).
5. **Who signs.** Attending staff of the visit sign (the lead and others). An admin who did not attend does not sign. The lead's valid signature is required to finalize; the lead or an admin starts the finalize. One valid signature per person per version: signing again while valid is 409 `already_signed`.
6. **What a signature records** (TRD §11.3): signer, role (`lead` or `attending`), the typed full name, the drawn PNG (checked: PNG magic bytes, at most 100 KB, at most 1200 x 600 px), the attestation text as shown, `signed_at` (server time), IP, user agent, `content_hash`, `content_version`. The attestation wording is one setting (`REPORT_ATTESTATION`, default from Q2). Signing needs a connection (OFF-10).
7. **Finalize is a saga that can be resumed**, as TRD §11.2, in the worker (`jobs/finalize.py`, job id `finalize:<report id>`): (1) `finalizing`, which blocks edits; (2) lock the visit; (3) read the facts again and compare hashes, and if they changed, unlock, return to `draft` with the signatures cleared and a `facts_changed` reason; (4) render the final PDF, store it under a key that is never reused (`visits/<report id>/final-v<n>.pdf`), compute `pdf_sha256`, and in **one transaction** set `final`, `finalized_at`, `finalized_by`, `content_hash`, `template_version`, and add `report.finalized` to the outbox. Rendering is retried 3 times with backoff; after the last failure the report is a `draft` again, the visit is unlocked, the signatures stay (the content did not change), `finalize_error` is stored and `report.finalize_failed` goes to the signer. A cron returns any report left in `finalizing` for 15 minutes to the saga.
8. **The lock is operations' and idempotent.** `POST /internal/visits/{id}/lock` takes `{report_id, report_version}`, answers the board's version, and a repeat with the same pair changes nothing; a different pair on a locked visit is 409. `unlock` is idempotent and also takes the pair, so a late unlock of an old version cannot open a visit locked by a newer one. A lock needs an ended visit (409 `not_ended` otherwise).
9. **The visit follows the report through events.** Operations consumes `report.finalized` (guarded by the report's version: `report_status = final`, `report_version`, `report_finalized_at`) and `report.reopened` (`report_status = draft`), and recomputes the request in the same transaction. The rest is `derive_status`, which is already built and tested: all visit reports final and no further visit needed gives Client Review; a reopened report in Client Review gives In progress and clears the review clock; after Closed the request stays Closed.
10. **Reopen** (admin, reason 5 to 500 characters): the final becomes `superseded`, a new draft with `version + 1` and the content copied is made (its narrative and selection kept, its signatures empty, facts re-read), the visit is unlocked, and `report.reopened` is emitted. A draft with a higher version already open is 409 `already_reopened`. Earlier versions stay readable and downloadable, marked superseded; a client sees final versions only, so a superseded one stays visible to them (they already received it) with the mark, and the newer draft stays hidden until final. [See question 5.]
11. **Reminders count working days** (Monday to Friday, no holiday calendar) in the zone of the visit's first station. At 2 working days after the visit ended with no final report, the attending staff who have not signed are told; at 5, the admins are told too. One reminder per report version per level (`reminders_sent`), reset by a reopen.
12. **Verify (RPT-11)** is public and rate-limited (the Nginx `auth` zone): `POST /api/v1/reports/verify` with `{sha256}` or an uploaded PDF (hashed on the server, at most 25 MB, never stored) answers `{number, version, status, finalized_at}` of the matching final or superseded version, or `{match: false}`. It returns no content. It looks up `pdf_sha256`.
13. **Clients and finals.** `GET /reports`, `GET /reports/{id}` and `download` already return final reports of the client's own organization. W17 adds the Report section on the visit page for clients (a final version: number, version, Download and the same sheet). The review page itself is W18. A client never sees a draft, a signature image, `finalize_error` or reminders (rule 6), and a draft asked for is 404 (rule 4).
14. **Access matrix.** Every new route goes in the matrix: patch content (attending, admin), sign (attending), finalize (lead, admin), reopen (admin), verify (public), the internal lock and unlock (service `report` only). A viewer never mutates (rule 5).

## 6. PWA pre-flight (answers)

1. **Device.** The editor and the signature dialog run on a phone and on a desktop; the lead may well sign on an iPhone. `signature_pad` draws on a canvas with pointer events: the canvas needs `touch-action: none` (otherwise iOS scrolls or zooms while drawing), and a size set from `devicePixelRatio`; rotating the phone clears a half-drawn signature, so the dialog locks its size while open and asks again if it is resized. The signed PDF opens in the app's sheet (W16), with Download as the fallback.
2. **Network.** Editing, signing, finalize and reopen need a connection (OFF-10; AFD §5.2 "This needs a connection"). Saves are debounced (800 ms) and keep going while the person types; a save that fails shows "Not saved yet. Retrying…" and the text stays in the field, in memory. `finalize` is a request that returns at once and the page follows the report until it is final or failed; asking again while `finalizing` queues nothing more. A `sign` repeated after a lost answer is 409 `already_signed`, and the page reads the record instead of showing an error.
3. **Lifecycle.** A new service worker waiting while someone signs: the update prompt is never applied while the dialog is open (the existing rule for forms; the dialog registers itself as busy). Nothing from the signature is queued in the outbox: a signature is never stored on the phone.
4. **Data and loss.** The drawn signature and the typed text live in memory only until `sign` is answered. Nothing is written to Dexie. Because the phone keeps no copy, closing the app mid-signature loses the drawing and nothing else. Edits already saved are on the server.
5. **Storage.** Signature PNGs are at most 100 KB in Mongo (at most a handful per report). Final PDFs are kept for ever; superseded ones too.
6. **Caching.** The service worker still caches no API response and no PDF. `signature_pad` (about 12 KB) is imported with `import()` in the signature dialog and added to `globIgnores`, so the precache stays at 29 of 30 files (the dialog needs a connection anyway). The editor joins the `app` chunk.
7. **Security.** A signature image is served only inside `GET /reports/{id}` to staff, never to a client, and never in an event or a log; the typed name and the attestation are stored as the person saw them. IP and user agent are stored for the audit and not shown to clients. `verify` returns no content and is rate limited. CSP is unchanged (`img-src` already allows `data:`).
8. **Proof.** Python: hash and canonical JSON, signature clearing (edit, facts change, identical facts keep them), a stale `content_version`, the saga with a fake renderer and a fake operations (every step failing once, a crash and resume, a changed fact at step 3, 3 retries then draft), the lock pair rule, `derive_status` for each case in decision 9, reminders under `ManualClock` across a weekend, verify, the matrix, the event contracts. The final PDF's text and baseline in CI. Playwright: J7 end to end (edit, sign as lead and as a second technician, a later edit clears both, finalize, the board is locked, the request is in Client Review, a client downloads the final, an admin reopens, the request is In progress); axe in light and dark; phone project for the signature pad (`@phone`). **Real devices:** an iPhone and an Android phone draw and send a signature (the W15 field-test visits give real drafts to sign).

## 7. Tests and exit checks

- **Exit (IP):** a technician signs on a phone and finalizes; the boards are locked (an offline phone that still holds edits gets a `locked` conflict that it can export); the request reaches Client Review when every visit report is final; an admin reopens one, the request returns to In progress and the corrected report is signed again.
- **No lost signature, no unsigned final:** a final report always has the lead's valid signature for exactly its `content_hash` (a database-level check in a test over every saga interruption point).
- **Immutability (rule 11):** no route changes a `final` or `superseded` report's content or signatures; a test calls each mutating route on a final.
- **Docs site:** status and badges, routes, the `signatures` schema, the six events, settings (`REPORT_ATTESTATION`, `REPORT_FINALIZE_RETRIES`, `REPORT_FINALIZING_TIMEOUT_S`, `REPORT_REMINDER_DAYS`, the Kafka key for operations), decisions W17-1 to W17-14, a changelog entry, and the P6 charts still on the list: the **finalize saga** (sequence, as built), the **report state** (draft, finalizing, final, superseded, with the transitions built), the **J7 swimlane** (draw.io: technician, lead, admin, report service, operations), the **signature invalidation decision tree**, and the **document flowchart** of who writes each part of the report. ## 8. Questions for you

1. **Q2 and Q3.** Is the attestation "I confirm the information in this report is accurate and the work described was carried out." right, and is a drawn signature with a typed name, attestation and audit record acceptable in every client jurisdiction? Default: yes to both (the PRD defaults); a certificate (PAdES) signature stays a later addition at step 4 of the saga.
2. **Staff-only elements (W17-1).** Print only shared elements in the report, drafts included? Default: **yes**. The alternative is a staff-only report PDF, which clients could not read.
3. **A lead who cannot sign.** If the lead has left or is away, the report cannot be finalized. Default: **no override in W17** (strict, as the PRD); an admin can reopen nothing that is not final, so the workaround would be a change of lead on the visit, which does not exist today. Alternative: an admin may finalize with another attending person's signature after typing a reason, recorded in the audit.
4. **Verify (RPT-11, "Could").** Build it in W17 (small: a lookup and a rate limit) or leave it to W18/Phase 8? Default: **build it**.
5. **What a client sees after a reopen.** The superseded version stays readable, marked Superseded, while the corrected draft is hidden until it is final. Default: as written. Alternative: hide the superseded version from the client as well and show "A corrected report is on its way."
6. **Where the editor lives.** AFD J7 gives it the route `/visit-reports/:id`, reached from the notification and from the Report section. Default: as in the AFD, a page of the Work section (Visits tab group), with the notification link moved from `/visits/:id` to it.
7. **Boards.** Draw the "AWS-MMS W17 screens" canvas (editor on desktop and phone, photo picker, signature dialog, finalizing and failed states, reopen dialog, client's final-report view; light and dark) and get it approved before slice 5? Default: **yes**, drawn while slices 1 and 2 are built.
8. **Working days (decision 11).** Monday to Friday in the first station's zone, no holiday calendar. Default: as written.
