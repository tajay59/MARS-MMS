# Phase 5 · W13 plan: the device store, the PIN session, prepare for field, offline station lookup

Proposed 2026-10-22. **Questions answered 2026-10-22** (section 8): all three proposed defaults stand. The mock-up boards (section 5) are drawn and approved before the screens of slices 2 to 4 are built, as for W9 to W11; slice 1 and the back end of slice 3 have no screens and start first.

Delivers OFF-1 (prepare), OFF-3 (offline data survives a restart), AUTH-12 (the device PIN, 30-day field session), QR-5 and CFG-4 offline (the scanned label and the latest configuration files open with no signal), OFF-9's data (`prepared_on`, the warnings in the visit dialogs) and the R4 check (iOS storage). Journey J5 is completed (its Prepare step) and J6's first step (unlock, scan) works. Depends on Phase 4 (merged: W12 close-out is #73).

**Not in W13** (so it is clear where it goes): the outbox, offline writes to the board, `visit.start`/`arrive`/`depart`/`end` offline, `/sync`, the conflict codes and Export, resumable uploads from Dexie, Background Sync, WebKit under Playwright (F6) and the kill-switch test (all W14); the field test (W15). In W13 an unlocked phone with no signal **reads** its prepared visits, stations, boards and files; it does not change them.

**Status (2026-10-22):** slice 1 is built on `feat/p5-w13-device-store` (not committed): `src/offline/{db,session,cleanup,account,storage}.ts`, `useLiveQuery`, the session store clears a user's prepared data on logout and when another user logs in. 100 offline Vitest tests, 501 in all; lint and type-check clean; the precache is still 29 files. `files` is keyed by `[visit_id+media_id]` (the same document can sit in two prepared visits), a change from the pwa skill's proposal; record it in BSD. The `persist()` call is made by Prepare in slice 3 (the helper `requestPersistence` is ready).

## 3. Decisions (proposed W13-1 to W13-14)

1. **One Dexie database, `aws-mms`, the six tables of TRD §7.1** (`bundles`, `files`, `visits`, `outbox`, `blobs`, `meta`), every row tagged `user_id` except `meta`'s device row. `outbox` is created now, with its strict local `seq` and unique `op_id`, but nothing writes to it until W14, so W14 adds no schema version. Indexes: the proposal in the pwa skill's `offline-data.md` §1; the final ones go in BSD "Device store". Dexie 4 is the only new runtime dependency; `fake-indexeddb` is dev only. `db.on('versionchange')` shows "AWS-MMS was updated in another tab. Reload to continue." and closes the connection. Upgrades are forward-only and never touch `outbox` or `blobs` rows.
2. **The device has an ID** made once (UUIDv7, `meta` key `device`) and sent with `field-bundle` so `prepared_on` can say where a visit is prepared. It identifies a browser profile, not a person; clearing site data makes a new one.
3. **The PIN** is 4 to 6 digits, set the first time a staff member who is not a viewer prepares a visit (AFD J5 step 4), and asked for again to change it on My profile. Verifier: PBKDF2-SHA-256, 310,000 iterations, a 16-byte random salt, 256 bits, through Web Crypto; compared in full. Stored in `meta` with the profile (`id`, `name`, `roles`, `org_id`, `theme`), `offline_until` (the refresh cookie's expiry, `refresh_expires_at`), the failure count and a `disabled` flag. **Never the PIN, never a token.** It is an access gate, not encryption (pwa `security.md` §2): at-rest protection is the phone's passcode, and the training checklist says so (Phase 8). A 4-digit PIN falls to anyone who copies the verifier out of IndexedDB; that is accepted and written in the TRD.
4. **The failure is counted before it is shown**: the count is written in Dexie, then the result renders, so killing the app cannot reset it. The fifth wrong PIN sets `disabled`: unlock is off until the next **online login by the same user**, data stays (AFD §5.2). A login by a different user replaces the profile (decision 5). Wrong-PIN feedback is "That PIN is not right. 2 tries left." (DBD voice), the lockout is "Connect to the internet and log in to continue. Your work is saved on this phone."
5. **One offline profile per device.** A staff login stores (or refreshes) the profile only when a PIN exists for **that same user**; a login by a different user deletes the first user's profile, PIN, `bundles`, `files` and `visits` (TRD §7.6) and keeps the first user's `outbox` and `blobs`, tagged with their `user_id`, which sync only after they log in again. Logout does the same for the current user and also sends the worker `CLEAR_RUNTIME_CACHES` (already done by the session store).
6. **When `/unlock` shows.** Only when the app starts with no live session **and** the refresh could not reach the server (`{kind: 'offline'}`) **and** an offline profile exists that is not disabled and not past `offline_until`. With signal, a valid cookie signs in as today and no PIN is asked. With signal and a dead cookie (`ended`), `/login`, as today. No profile, or an expired one, with no signal: `/login`, which says "No connection" (AFD §5.2). `?next=` is kept, so the unlock lands on the page the person was on.
7. **Unlocked is not signed in.** After a correct PIN the session store holds the cached profile with `offline: true` and **no access token**. Route guards read roles from it; API calls are not made. The app tries a silent refresh whenever `online` fires or the app returns to the foreground: on success the token arrives and `offline` goes false with no screen change; on a 401 the session ends with its reason, the data stays, and `/login` explains. The offline state is a plain flag on the existing store, not a second session.
8. **What opens with no signal** (OFF-10): `/unlock`, `/scan`, `/s/:token`, `/stations/:id` (the field view, from the bundle), `/visits/:id` and `/visits/:id/field` for a prepared visit (read only in W13), and `/sync`. Everything else shows the shared **"This needs a connection"** state (AFD §5.2) with a way back. Clients have no offline session (AUTH-12): a client who is offline sees it on every page.
9. **The field bundle** (`GET /api/v1/visits/{id}/field-bundle?device_id=…`, attending staff and admin; 404 outside scope, 409 `visit_not_preparable` unless the visit is `planned` or `started`) is one JSON document with `bundle_version: 1`, `prepared_at`, `app_min_version` and:
   - the visit (as `GET /visits/{id}` for staff, with `stations[].owner`), and its request (reference, title, description, priority, type, the stations);
   - for each station: the full station record staff see (with `qr_token`, time zone, location), its installed sensors with their models, the calibration status (due dates, the latest calibration), the **latest configuration version** and its file descriptors, the station's documents and its installed models' documents as descriptors `{id, name, type, size, content_type}`, and the visit's **board** for that station (all elements, with their versions);
   - a flat `files[]` list of everything that can be downloaded (descriptor plus the endpoint that gives the ten-minute link), each marked `required` (the latest configuration version's files) or `optional` (documents).
   The route also appends `{user_id, device_id, at}` to `site_visits.prepared_on` (replacing an earlier entry of the same user and device, at most 10 entries) and emits `visit.prepared` in the same transaction (rule 2). It does not bump the visit's state or version for anything else. Preparing again is how a technician takes in changes.
10. **Files are downloaded by the browser, one at a time, as blobs** into `files` (`media_id`, `user_id`, `visit_id`, `station_id`, `name`, `size`, `type`, `blob`, `sha256?`), through the ten-minute links the existing endpoints give, with the size checked against the descriptor and **never a URL stored** (TRD §7.1). A failed file keeps the others; Prepare reports "Ready, but 2 files are missing" with Try again, and a visit is marked ready only when the bundle and every `required` file are stored. Progress counts bytes. The download runs in the page (iOS stops background JavaScript; the screen says "Keep AWS-MMS open") with a Screen Wake Lock where the browser has one (F12).
11. **Ready offline** is a state of `bundles` (`prepared_at`, `bundle_version`, `ready: true`, the file counts), shown as a badge on the visit with "Prepared 2 days ago" and an **Update offline copy** action. The visit page also shows "Changed since you prepared" when the server's `updated_at` for the visit is newer than `prepared_at` (owner changed, rescheduled, cancelled), using the visit it already fetched. Nothing refreshes a bundle by itself: a bundle that silently changed under a technician's feet is worse than an old one.
12. **iPhone (F2).** Prepare in a Safari tab shows the install steps (Share, Add to Home Screen, open it from the Home Screen, log in again, then Prepare) and does not prepare, because the Home Screen app's storage is separate from Safari's. Android and desktop Chrome prepare in a tab. Install is detected with `matchMedia('(display-mode: standalone)')` and `navigator.standalone`. Android gets an **Install** button from `beforeinstallprompt` (kept in a store), shown once on the dashboard and always on My profile.
13. **Offline station lookup** (QR-5): `/s/:token` looks the token up in the stored bundles' stations (`qr_token`); a match opens `/stations/:id` from the bundle (the field view: details, sensors, calibration, the latest configuration and its documents opened from `files` with `URL.createObjectURL`, revoked when the view closes). No match: **"This station is not on your phone. Open it with a connection, or prepare a visit that includes it."** A label that was replaced after the bundle was made still resolves offline (the server answers 410 only online); the screen cannot know, and it is the same limit as any printed label, noted in the TRD.
14. **Sizes and storage.** The bundle JSON for a visit with several stations is well under 1 MB; the weight is the files. `persist()` is requested on first field use (the Prepare button's first run); `/sync` shows used, free, persisted and the under-1-GB warning it has. Prepare estimates the download first (sum of `size`) and asks before a download over 100 MB on a link the browser calls `saveData` or cellular (`navigator.connection`, where it exists).

## 6. PWA pre-flight (answers)

1. **Device.** iOS Safari installed is the floor: a separate store from Safari (F2), `persist()` may be false (F3), JavaScript stops in the background (F12, so Prepare says "Keep AWS-MMS open"), no `beforeinstallprompt`. Android Chrome and desktop Chrome and Edge get the same code. The PBKDF2 and Web Crypto calls are in every supported browser; the Web Lock is already relied on.
2. **Network.** Prepare is online only and is interrupted-safe: files already stored are skipped on the next run (matched by `media_id` and size), the bundle is written in one transaction after the JSON arrives, and a bundle counts as ready only after the required files are in. Unlock needs no network. A refresh that fails with a 5xx is `offline`, not `ended` (already so), so a restarting server never signs a technician out.
3. **Lifecycle.** The Dexie schema starts at version 1 with every table, so W14 adds no migration; `bundle_version` and `app_min_version` let a newer app read an older bundle and refuse a newer one with "Update the app, then prepare again". The update prompt is not applied while a prepare is running (the update store is told, as it is for an open form). Ops are not queued in W13, so F8 (op payload versions) is a W14 job.
4. **Data and loss.** The truth in W13 is the server; the bundle is a read copy, deleted on logout or change of user, and replaced by preparing again. The one thing that must not be lost is a user's `outbox` and `blobs`: no W13 code deletes them, and a test asserts it for logout and for a second user.
5. **Storage.** Config files and documents are the weight (megabytes each). The storage check (question 2) puts 50 photos and 3 videos in `blobs`. `persist()` is requested at the first Prepare; the result is shown, not assumed (F3).
6. **Caching.** Nothing new in the worker. No API response is cached; the bundle lives in Dexie. Presigned URLs are used once and never stored.
7. **Security and shared devices.** No token in storage; the profile holds no email or phone; `files` and `bundles` carry `user_id` and every read filters by it; logout and a user change delete them; a test with two users covers it. CSP: Web Crypto, IndexedDB and `blob:` object URLs need no change (`img-src` and `media-src` have `blob:`; confirm `object-src` and the PDF viewer for an offline PDF, which loads from a `blob:` URL: the PDF viewer chunk is outside the precache (F17), so **offline PDFs are not viewable in W13**; the file opens through the share sheet or a download instead; decided in question 1).
8. **Proof.** Vitest with `fake-indexeddb` (schema, upgrade, PIN, lockout across a "restart", cleanup, bundle write and resume); API tests and the authorization matrix for the bundle route against real Mongo; Playwright against the production build with `setOffline` in Chromium (prepare, go offline, reload, unlock, scan, open the station and its file; wrong PIN five times; logout then another user; axe on `/unlock` in light and dark; the phone project). Real devices: the Android check at the end of slice 4, and the **W13 iOS check** (testing.md §5) when the iPhone is available.

## 8. Questions for you (answered 2026-10-22: the defaults stand)

1. **Which files does Prepare download?** Default: the latest configuration version's files always (they are the point of CFG-4), and the station's and installed models' documents as a **checklist with sizes**, with the ones up to 10 MB ticked. Photos already on a board are not downloaded (their places show "Photo available when online"). Offline PDFs open through the phone's own viewer (share or download), because the in-app viewer is not in the precache (F17). Alternative: tick nothing by default, or download every document.
2. **The W13 iOS storage check.** Default: build a small **Storage check** on `/sync` (staff only): pick up to 60 photos and videos from the camera or library, they are stored in `blobs` marked as a test, and after a restart Verify re-reads and re-hashes each one; a button clears them. It proves R4 on a phone now, two weeks before the field test, and W14's real capture reuses the same blob store. Alternative: skip the tool and run the check in W14 when real capture exists (the iOS risk is found a week later).
3. **Updating a prepared visit.** Default: manual, with "Prepared 2 days ago" and "Changed since you prepared" on the visit (decision 11). Alternative: also refresh the bundle automatically at the start of each online session while the visit is `planned` and nothing has been recorded on the phone. Riskier for the technician who is relying on what they saw, so not proposed.
